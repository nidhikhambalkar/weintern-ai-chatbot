# Chapter 1 - Company Overview

## About WeIntern
WeIntern is an EdTech company focused on helping students and freshers build industry-ready skills through practical learning. It provides internship programs, professional training, live projects, career guidance, and placement assistance across various technology and business domains.

The learning approach at WeIntern emphasizes hands-on experience instead of only theoretical knowledge. Students work on real-world projects, receive mentorship from industry professionals, and improve both their technical and professional skills.

The goal of WeIntern is to bridge the gap between academic education and industry requirements by preparing learners for successful careers.


## Vision
Our vision is to become a trusted learning and career development platform that empowers students and professionals with industry-relevant skills, practical experience, and career opportunities. WeIntern aims to bridge the gap between education and employment by making quality training accessible to every learner.

## Mission
Our mission is to provide practical, industry-oriented education through internships, live projects, mentorship, and career support. WeIntern is committed to helping learners gain real-world experience, improve employability, and build successful careers in technology and business.


## What WeIntern Does
WeIntern provides industry-focused internship programs and professional training designed to help students and freshers gain practical skills. The platform offers learning opportunities in multiple domains, including Full Stack Development, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Python Development, Java Development, Digital Marketing, UI/UX Design, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, and more.

Students learn through structured training, work on live industry projects, receive guidance from mentors, build professional portfolios, prepare for interviews, and receive placement assistance to improve their career opportunities. WeIntern follows a Learn → Build → Earn approach that focuses on practical experience and industry readiness.
