# Chapter 4 - Internship

## Internship Overview
The internship is designed to give students real project experience in a structured and guided setting. It combines learning, execution, mentorship, and evaluation to help students understand how work is done in a professional environment.

## Internship Domains
The internship is available across multiple domains such as software development, AI, data science, digital marketing, UI/UX, and related technology-focused areas. Students can choose a domain that aligns with their career interest.

## Internship Duration
The duration of the internship depends on the specific program and learning structure. It is usually designed to give enough time for students to understand the domain, complete tasks, and build practical experience.

## Internship Process
The internship process usually begins with registration, followed by selection, onboarding, learning support, project execution, task submission, and ongoing evaluation. This flow helps learners stay organized and progress step by step.

## Registration Process
Registration is the first step where students provide their basic details and indicate interest in the relevant internship program. Once registered, they follow the program’s official communication and onboarding flow.

## Selection Process
Selection depends on the program’s eligibility and internal screening process. It is usually meant to identify students who are a good match for the chosen domain and learning path.

## Onboarding
Onboarding introduces students to the internship workflow, communication channels, expectations, and initial learning tasks. It helps new participants understand what to do and how to get started.

## Live Projects
Live projects are a core part of the internship because they give students hands-on experience with real-world problem-solving. Working on projects helps learners build practical confidence and a stronger portfolio.

## Daily Tasks
Daily tasks may include learning updates, project progress, assignment completion, collaboration, and mentor check-ins. These tasks help students stay active and develop discipline in a professional setup.

## Attendance
Attendance is usually tracked to ensure participation and consistency in the learning experience. Students are encouraged to stay regular so they can complete the internship smoothly.

## Performance Evaluation
Performance is generally evaluated through the quality of work, project outcomes, task completion, and participation. This helps mentors assess readiness and provide constructive feedback.

## Team Collaboration
Team collaboration is an important part of the internship because students learn to communicate, coordinate, and contribute to project goals. It gives them a closer understanding of how real work happens in a team-driven environment.

## Internship Rules
Internship rules usually cover attendance, communication, professionalism, deadlines, and conduct expectations. These rules help maintain a disciplined and productive learning environment.
