# Chapter 7 - Fees

## Fee Structure
The fee structure depends on the selected program, its duration, and the current offer details available at the time of enrollment. Students should review the official program information before making a decision.

## Course-wise Fees
Course-wise fees may vary because each program has its own learning scope, duration, and support model. It is important to compare the features of each track before paying.

## Payment Methods
Payment methods are shared through the official enrollment process and may include digital payment options approved by the platform. Students should confirm the latest available methods before proceeding.

## EMI Options
EMI options, if available, are usually tied to the current program offer and payment terms. These options can help make fee planning more flexible for students.

## Discounts
Discounts may be offered during certain enrollment periods or for selected programs. Students should check official announcements to avoid relying on outdated information.

## Refund Policy
The refund policy explains the conditions under which a fee payment may be refundable. Students should read the policy carefully before enrolling to understand the rules and timelines.

## Scholarships
Scholarships or fee support can help reduce the financial burden for eligible students. Availability usually depends on the opportunity cycle and the official support process.
