# Chapter 5 - Certification

## Certificate Overview
Certification at WeIntern is meant to recognize the learner’s completion of a selected learning path and related project outcomes. It serves as proof of participation in structured learning and practical experience.

## Types of Certificates
The type of certificate depends on the program and completion requirements associated with that particular learning track. Students should refer to the relevant program information for the exact certificate type.

## Certificate Eligibility
Eligibility is typically based on successful participation in the assigned program and satisfaction of the required completion conditions. Students are advised to understand the criteria before enrolling or expecting a credential.

## Completion Criteria
Completion criteria usually include finishing the required training, assignments, and project-related outcomes. This ensures that learners are rewarded for demonstrated participation and progress.

## Verification
Verification helps confirm that a certificate is authentic and corresponds to the actual program completion. Students can use the official credential process or support route if they need to verify their certification details.

## Certificate Validity
The validity of a certificate depends on the specific program and the official way in which the credential is issued. Students are encouraged to confirm details from the certified source.

## LinkedIn Usage
Students can usually add their certificate to LinkedIn or resume-based profiles to highlight their learning and skill development. This helps present their experience more professionally to recruiters and employers.
