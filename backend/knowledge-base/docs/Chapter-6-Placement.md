# Chapter 6 - Placement Assistance

## Placement Support
Placement support is designed to help students improve their readiness for internship and job opportunities. It focuses on practical preparation, communication, and professional presentation.

## Hiring Partners
Hiring partners are part of the wider placement ecosystem that can help students connect with potential opportunities. Their involvement depends on the active opportunities and program collaboration at a given time.

## Placement Process
The placement process usually includes preparing the learner profile, improving resume quality, participating in interview practice, and receiving guidance for job applications. It is meant to make students more confident in the hiring journey.

## Resume Review
Resume review helps students improve how they present their skills, projects, learning, and achievements. A clearer and stronger resume can improve the chances of getting noticed by recruiters.

## Mock Interviews
Mock interviews help students practice answering common interview questions in a guided setting. This improves confidence, communication, and performance under real interview conditions.

## Job Assistance
Job assistance includes guidance around applications, profile readiness, and use of the support system during the job search process. It helps students understand the next steps more clearly.

## Career Counseling
Career counseling supports students in understanding career direction, choosing the right opportunities, and improving decision-making related to future growth. It is a helpful step for learners who want more clarity on their path.
