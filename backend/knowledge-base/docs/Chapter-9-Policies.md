# Chapter 9 - Policies

## Terms & Conditions
The terms and conditions define the formal rules, responsibilities, and expectations for students participating in the program. These conditions are important for understanding the official agreement between the learner and the platform.

## Attendance Policy
The attendance policy explains the participation expectations for live sessions, structured learning, and internship activities. It helps maintain consistency and accountability in the learning process.

## Code of Conduct
The code of conduct outlines the expected professional behavior, respect for peers and mentors, and communication standards during the program. It ensures a disciplined and safe learning environment.

## Privacy Policy
The privacy policy explains how student information is collected, managed, and protected. It helps learners understand the official privacy commitments and data-handling practices.

## Refund Policy
The refund policy describes the conditions under which fee-related refunds may be requested and processed. Students should review this policy before enrolling to understand the limitations and timelines.

## Certificate Policy
The certificate policy describes how certificates are issued, what completion conditions apply, and how credentials are presented or verified. It gives students clarity on the certificate process.

## Placement Policy
The placement policy explains how the placement support process works and what students should expect from the program’s career assistance flow. It helps set clear expectations around job-related support.

## FAQs

**Answer:**
Terms and conditions me program participation, responsibilities, aur official rules define hote hain jo students ko follow karne chahiye.

Attendance policy me live sessions aur program participation ke liye required discipline aur presence expectations define hote hain.
