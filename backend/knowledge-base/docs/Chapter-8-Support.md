# Chapter 8 - Student Support

## Help Desk
The help desk acts as the first point of contact for student queries and general support needs. It helps learners get quick direction on common problems and official communication steps.

## Technical Support
Technical support helps with issues related to platform access, login, learning tools, and other technical difficulties that may affect the internship experience. It ensures students can continue their learning without unnecessary disruption.

## Academic Support
Academic support helps students with learning-related concerns, understanding assignments, and staying on track with course or project work. This support is useful for improving clarity and confidence.

## WhatsApp Support
WhatsApp support may be used for faster communication and quick assistance when students need immediate guidance. It is often helpful for time-sensitive queries and enrollment-related questions.

## Email Support
Email support is a formal way to communicate complex issues, document follow-up, and receive detailed assistance. It is useful when a conversation needs a clear written record.

## Contact Information
Contact information helps students reach the right support channel for enrollment, technical help, academic questions, or issue escalation. This information should always be checked from the official source.

## Office Hours
Office hours define when support representatives are available for assistance. Students should use the posted schedule to know when they can expect a response or follow-up.

## Escalation Process
The escalation process is used when a regular support query needs higher-level attention or a more formal review. This helps ensure unresolved issues are moved to the correct team for action.
